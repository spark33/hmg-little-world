## Notice review and rights gates

All five notices linked from the actual [notice index](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/notice) were readable; none remained inaccessible. The noncanonical `/notices` URL did not resolve and is not evidence of an unavailable official notice.

- [Technical scope notice](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/notice/c0a1a000-2026-4923-8000-000000000008): use documented SDKs/APIs. A portal “preparing” label does not by itself prohibit emulator development when the public documentation provides instructions. Undocumented functions and Pleos LLM are unavailable; external speech-recognition services receiving the vehicle microphone are unsupported. ROAMY does not implement microphone, location or lighting functions, and must state that as its implementation scope.
- [Submission clarification](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/notice/c0a1a000-2026-4923-8000-000000000007): additional PPT/PDF/HTML materials are allowed; secrets and private API keys need not be published. Preserve the entrant's selected source cut and release evidence.
- [Entry/IP notice](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/notice/c0a1a000-2026-4923-8000-000000000006): entrants must own or lawfully use the work; plagiarism, a previously awarded entry from another contest, inaccurate claims and rights violations create exclusion/revocation risk. Hyundai Motor Group officers/employees may participate but are excluded from awards. Applicants retain IP subject to possible negotiated non-exclusive licensing and a four-month priority-negotiation provision for IP transfer; the owner must review the complete terms before signing.
- [Recruitment notice](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/notice/c0a1a000-2026-4923-8000-000000000001) and [briefing notice](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/notice/c0a1a000-2026-4923-8000-000000000005) were also checked. They do not add a mandatory initial video or real-vehicle requirement.

No explicit generative-AI prohibition or blanket permission was found in these five notices, the reviewed guide or [site terms](https://appdevchallenge.auto-hmg.io/terms). That is **not AI approval or a legal originality determination**. The project uses AI-assisted implementation; the entrant must review authorship, originality, prior-contest history and permissions and disclose assistance accurately where requested. Keep truthful provenance rather than claiming unaided authorship.

The terms require lawful use and open-source attribution. ROAMY's procedural geometry/audio avoids downloaded art, but that does not establish clearance for every dependency, font, data item or landmark depiction. Review the build-generated runtime dependency inventory and bundled license texts using **Settings → Open-source licenses**; verify their completeness and source/license provenance in the submitted build before accepting that gate. The absence of an own-source LICENSE is not itself a contest-rule violation; it is separate from third-party obligations. The Pleos SDK's license and redistribution requirements also require owner review; do not equate a successful Maven download with unrestricted redistribution rights. Competition acceptance, an award and Pleos App Market approval are separate events.

The form separates operational identity from the anonymous project narrative and asks entrants to avoid identity leakage in shared materials. Its APK naming convention also uses the team/individual entrant label. Keep the authorized name in the required operational fields/file name, omit it from the product pitch, and have the owner confirm how these instructions apply to linked filenames and repository metadata. This audit did not contact the organizer or certify anonymity.
